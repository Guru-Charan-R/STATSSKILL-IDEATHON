## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated authority of an elite economic institute, data observatory, or specialized scientific publication. The interface operates as an interactive analytical journal, merging classic intellectual typography with state-of-the-art telemetry and scientific data visualization.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Scholarly Authority:** Evokes peer-reviewed academic prestige, high-level macroeconomic intelligence, and rigorous empirical truth.
- **Instrumental Precision:** High-density data layers, micro-metric readouts, monospaced tabular benchmarks, and sharp vector visualizations.
- **Dark Editorial Nocturne:** Deep obsidian and nocturnal oceanic slates replace stark pure blacks, preserving eye endurance during prolonged analysis while rendering luminous accents with vivid clarity.
- **Controlled Radiance:** Color serves exclusively as informational telemetry—denoting variance, significance, momentum, and statistical confidence intervals against a solemn backdrop.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is constructed upon nocturnal oceanic strata, layered systematically to produce optical depth without relying on decorative elevation.

### Structural Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`#070d17`):** The foundational substrate for page canvases and deep viewport envelopes.
- **Surface Low (`#0b1526`):** Secondary canvas, side rails, and recessed data trays.
- **Surface Container (`#0f1d33`):** Default card panels, editorial modules, and analytical containers.
- **Surface High (`#16243b`):** Elevated overlays, hovering metrics panels, flyout drawers, and active table rows.
- **Surface Highest (`#1d2e4a`):** Active selections, pill buttons, highlighted interactive tokens, and tooltips.

### Structural Lines & Borders
- **Border Subtle (`#1e293b`):** Primary structural framing, subtle gridlines, dividing rules, and card outlines.
- **Border Prominent (`#334155`):** Focused states, active tab indicators, table header bounds, and radar chart perimeter axes.

### Telemetry Accents
- **Primary Telemetry Cyan (`#2dd4bf`):** Primary signals, current run indicators, active trajectories, positive trend deltas, and focal focal points.
- **Secondary Telemetry Sky (`#38bdf8`):** Comparative cohorts, baseline indexes, secondary chart series, and informational callouts.
- **Deep Signal Teal (`#14b8a6`):** Confidence envelopes, area fills (at low opacity), and historical cohort lines.

### Text & Editorial Contrast
- **Editorial Heading (`#f1f5f9`):** Authoritative high-contrast off-white for serifs, titles, and principal numeric callouts.
- **Primary Body (`#cbd5e1`):** Neutral slate with high legibility for analytical prose and documentation.
- **De-emphasized / Metadata (`#94a3b8`):** Captions, timestamps, axis ticks, and unit legends.
- **Muted Rules / Inactive (`#475569`):** Disabled values, dormant ticks, and secondary data guides.

## Typography

The typography system relies on a functional tri-font architecture designed to mirror an institutional research whitepaper:
- **Newsreader (Editorial Serif):** Used for analytical headlines, section theses, and lead quotations. Infuses academic dignity and intellectual depth into empirical figures.
- **Public Sans (Institutional Grotesque):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse font used for narrative breakdowns, methodology explanations, documentation, and standard UI interactions.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry Monospace):** Precise tabular-numeric engine powering chart legends, coordinate matrices, p-value readouts, badges, timestamps, and statistical axes.

Numerical tables must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict columnar vertical alignment across financial and scientific tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an asymmetric publication architecture: a structured 12-column analytical grid flanked by an optional metadata spine.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid. Main narrative and primary telemetry visualizer span 8 columns; ancillary metadata, historical reference parameters, and methodology footnotes occupy the remaining 4 columns. Outer margin: `3.5rem` (`margin-lg`), Column gutter: `2rem` (`gutter-lg`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Complex chart visualizations occupy full width; analytical text collapses to single-column narrative flow. Outer margin: `2rem` (`margin`), Column gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column compact flow. Visualizations collapse to vertically scrollable cards; horizontal data matrices convert into swipeable tabular reels. Outer margin: `1rem` (`margin-sm`), Column gutter: `1rem` (`gutter-sm`).

### Rhythm & Density
Spacing reflects the dense yet legible nature of scientific reporting. Data panels employ compact vertical padding (`space-md`) paired with generous structural separation between macro report modules (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, floating drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tectonic surface stratification, low-contrast structural outlines, and selective back-glow telemetry.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Recessed / Base Canvas:** `#070d17` creates maximum depth for viewport bounds and underlying chart canvas grounds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#0f1d33` outlined with a 1px solid border in `#1e293b`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover States & Sub-panels):** `#16243b` with a 1px solid border in `#334155`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Drawers & Detail Inspection Panels):** `#1d2e4a` framed with `#334155` and accompanied by a razor-thin top perimeter line in `#2dd4bf` (at 40% opacity).

### Optical Glow & Luminescence
- **Telemetry Glow:** Critical alerts, active telemetry markers, and focused data points project an ambient blur rather than a drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.22)`.
- **Chart Radar Blurs:** Active radar polylines and target nodes project a tight vector illumination: `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4))`.

## Shapes

The design system employs an architectural, near-orthogonal geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are subtly softened to eliminate harsh raw edges without compromising the precision expected of an analytical instrument.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard Cards, Panels, & Viewports:** `0.25rem` (soft) corner radius, reinforcing crisp tabular divisions.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Selectors, Buttons):** `0.25rem` corner radius.
- **Metric Chips & Telemetry Tags:** `0.25rem` corner radius or strict sharp styling for micro-tags.
- **Floating Overlays & Dialogs:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius maximum.
- **Radar & Radial Visualizations:** Perfect circular concentric geometric rings nested within orthogonal frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Telemetry Action:** Solid background in `#2dd4bf`, text in `#070d17` (bold `Public Sans`), 0.25rem radius. Hover state transitions to `#38bdf8` with a subtle luminous cyan glow (`0 0 14px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Institutional Outline:** Surface in `#0f1d33`, border in 1px `#334155`, text in `#f1f5f9`. Hover induces background shift to `#16243b` and border shift to `#2dd4bf`.
- **Tertiary / Utility Button:** Borderless, text in `#94a3b8` (Newsreader or JetBrains Mono uppercase), hovering to `#cbd5e1` with a cyan underline.

### Data Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Status Badges:** JetBrains Mono, uppercase `label-sm`, tracking `0.05em`. Background tinted at 10% opacity of the telemetry hue with a matching 1px border at 30% opacity (e.g., background `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.1)`, border `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.3)`, text `#2dd4bf`).
- **Live Indicator Tag:** Prepended by a pulsing 6px circular radar dot with a cyan ping wave.

### Data Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Frame:** `#0f1d33` container framed with 1px border in `#1e293b`.
- **Header:** Features a serif section title in `#f1f5f9` juxtaposed with a monospace parameter identifier in `#94a3b8` (e.g., `SERIES: 04-A // CONFIDENCE: 98.4%`).
- **Content:** Deep `#0b1526` recessed chart area with subtle 1px dashed grid lines in `#1e293b`.

### Radar & Telemetry Visualizations
- **Web / Spoke Structure:** Concentric polygon rings rendered in `#1e293b` with primary axis vectors highlighted in `#334155`.
- **Data Polygon 1 (Primary):** Stroke in `#2dd4bf` (1.5px), fill in `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.12)`, vertex nodes rendered as sharp 4px square markers in `#2dd4bf` with cyan radial glow.
- **Data Polygon 2 (Baseline/Historical):** Stroke in `#38bdf8` (1px, dashed `3 3`), fill in `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.05)`.
- **Legend & Coordinates:** Positioned around radar perimeters using `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm` in `#94a3b8`.

### Input Fields & Filter Bars
- **Text & Metric Input:** Background `#0b1526`, border 1px solid `#1e293b`, text `#f1f5f9`, placeholder `#475569`. On focus: border shifts crisply to `#2dd4bf`, with box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #2dd4bf`.
- **Select & Segmented Switch:** Monospaced, enclosed in an outer border of `#1e293b`. Active segments receive `#1d2e4a` background with `#2dd4bf` text and a luminous lower border indicator.

### Tables & Statistical Lists
- **Header:** Uppercase `label-sm` in `JetBrains Mono`, text color `#94a3b8`, baseline border 1px solid `#334155`.
- **Row:** Alternating or clean borders using `#1e293b`. Hover transitions the row background to `#16243b`.
- **Numeric Cells:** Formatted in tabular-numeric `JetBrains Mono`, right-aligned, with positive values in `#2dd4bf` and negative variance in `#f87171`.
