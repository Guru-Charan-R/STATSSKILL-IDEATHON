## Brand & Style

This design system blends institutional credibility with high-velocity modern SaaS clarity. Tailored for enterprise and government statistical operations, the personality communicates authoritative confidence, pedagogical clarity, and AI-enabled efficiency. It is designed to replace bureaucratic friction with crisp, delightful analytical tools.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate Precision** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- Bright, clinical surfaces balanced by profound deep navy structural anchor sections.
- Radial luminous backdrops that suggest active computation without compromising data density.
- Generous corner radii, floating tactical pills, and crisp micro-accents (glowing indicators, telemetry progress meters) that make quantitative training approachable and engaging.

## Colors

The palette establishes clear optical weight between institutional grounding and computational agility:

- **Primary (`#0f172a` / `#081a34`):** Midnight Navy acts as the primary text, dark action anchor, and foundation for immersive feature panels. It communicates enterprise reliability.
- **Secondary (`#0284c7`):** Vibrant Cerulean drives interaction states, primary buttons, hyperlinks, and metric focus points.
- **Tertiary (`#00a8b5` / `#00bcd4`):** Luminous Cyan-Teal is dedicated to AI intelligence signals, competency highlights, progress gradient endpoints, and live status dots.
- **Neutral (`#f8fafc` / `#f4f8fc`):** An airy, cool slate base tint preventing eye fatigue during lengthy training sessions.

### Functional Gradients & Accents
- **Dark Feature Canvas:** Multi-point radial linear gradient blending from `#05263b` to `#091a28`, overlaid with a 5% opacity grid for data-terminal aesthetics.
- **Luminous Glows:** Subtle ambient radial washes (`rgba(0, 188, 212, 0.12)` into `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`) deployed under hero cards and high-priority learning metrics.
- **Status & Feedback:** Amber warning (`#f59e0b` on `#fef3c7`) for near-ready competencies, Emerald success (`#10b981` on `#ecfdf5`) for passed assessments.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual sans-serif system:

1. **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric forms with balanced stroke contrast lend a progressive, friendly edge to institutional subject matter. Display styles employ tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a cohesive punchline appearance.
2. **Text & System Interface (Inter):** Highly legible across varied hardware screens, ensuring formulas, statistical abbreviations, and tabular parameters read cleanly without misinterpretation.
3. **Eyebrows & Section Markers:** Set in uppercase `eyebrow` styling using `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded tracking (`0.12em`) in secondary cerulean for structured orientation.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by maximum container widths to safeguard visual cohesion across widescreen displays:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max container width of 1280px, 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and dynamic outer margins scaling from 32px up to automatic centering.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px margins. Two-column card rows fold into single-column or balanced double blocks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 16px page margins. Interactive cards span full width.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical pacing relies on a strict 8px increment system (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`).
- Module sections maintain `4rem` to `6rem` vertical separation to let light-toned surfaces breathe.
- Metric clusters and card grids prioritize internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to enforce comfortable target boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft diffusion rather than stark separation to uphold a clean, luminous aesthetic:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background slate surface (`#f8fafc`) featuring ambient mesh highlights using large blurred radial ellipses (`blur: 80px`).
- **Elevated Cards (Level 1):** Solid white (`#ffffff`) surfaces defined by a crisp hair-thin perimeter outline (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`) coupled with an expansive, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 10px 30px -5px rgba(8, 26, 52, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(8, 26, 52, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Modals & Interactive Badges (Level 2):** Elevated floating widgets (e.g., live quiz verification chips, AI contextual hints) use `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(8, 26, 52, 0.12)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Dark Structural Containers:** Enclosed feature wrappers use high-contrast tonal containment (`#05263b` to `#091a28`), framed with an ultra-thin cyan border (`1px solid rgba(0, 188, 212, 0.2)`) and inset grid detailing.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous geometry that softens the rigid character typical of statistical data:

- **Surface Panels & Main Cards:** Built with a smooth `20px` to `24px` radius (`rounded-xl`), creating floating physical slates.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Structured at `10px` to `12px` radius (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`) to preserve crisp actionable affordances.
- **Metadata Tags & Status Badges:** Fully circular pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to immediately isolate contextual indicators from surrounding square-corner data structures.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Micro-containers for functional glyphs leverage `12px` to `14px` soft rounded squares, accented by subtle cyan-tinted fills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Gradient cerulean-to-teal background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0284c7 0%, #00a8b5 100%)`), white bold text, pill or `12px` border radius, subtle glow shadow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`). Includes inline trailing arrow icons for progression.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pure white background or transparent slate with a `1px` border (`#e2e8f0`), `#0f172a` text, shifting on hover to `#f1f5f9` with zero translation.
- **Dark Inverted Actions:** On midnight containers, buttons use solid `#00bcd4` text on crisp translucent white bases (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), transitioning to full solid teal fills.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- **Glowing Status Indicators:** Fully rounded capsule wrappers featuring a `6px` circular dot with a soft animated pulse ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 188, 212, 0.3)`).
- **Categorical Chips:** Muted light backgrounds (`#ecfeff` or `#f0f9ff`) with colored bold micro-labels (`12px`) used for track tags (e.g., "Smart India Hackathon", "Unique to StatSkill").

### Interactive Cards & Content Blocks
- **Feature Cards:** Pure `#ffffff` containers with `24px` corner radii, internal `space-lg` padding, and a circular top-left icon medallion (`44px` diameter with an azure background). Micro-typography pairs bold title leads with readable gray descriptive copy.
- **Dark Feature Section:** Deep teal-navy wrapper featuring dual-column layouts: descriptive editorial on the left, interactive task readiness simulations on the right.

### Metric Meters & Progress Bars
- **Telemetry Bars:** Contained inside smooth tracks (`8px` height, `#1e293b` on dark, `#f1f5f9` on light). Active fills apply continuous horizontal gradients (`#0284c7` to `#00bcd4`) with rounded caps and right-aligned bold percentage readouts.

### List Items & Assessment Checkers
- **Readiness Checklist Items:** Inline flex rows carrying custom circular check icons with dual-layer cyan strokes, accompanied by regular body type and subtle bottom dividers for clean scannability.
